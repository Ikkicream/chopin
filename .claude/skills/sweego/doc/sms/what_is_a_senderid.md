# What is a SenderId?

Source : https://learn.sweego.io/docs/sms/what_is_a_senderid

> With Sweego, you have the possibility of sending SMS with a custom Sender ID, which will help your customers recognize you.

With Sweego, you have the possibility of sending SMS with a custom Sender ID, which will help your customers recognize you.

You can define a custom Sender ID from the Sweego application. From the homepage of the application, click the card “SMS with Sweego”, which will take you to the “Setup SMS” page. Then, click on the “Sender ID” tab.

![senderid1](undefined)

Click on the button to add a Sender ID. In the panel, you can define the name of your Sender ID.

Rules for Sender IDs:

- Must be between 3 and 11 characters.

- Only letters and numbers are allowed. No special characters.

- Must contain at least 1 letter.

![senderid1](undefined)

Once you’ve entered your custom Sender ID, it will be sent to our team for verification. It takes between an hour and a few days to have your Sender ID verified (depends when we receive your request, as we have working hours in France, and don’t work on weekends). We check each Sender ID in an effort to prevent SMS fraud.

Please know that you can still send SMS before your Sender ID is verified! Or if you choose to send SMS without a custom Sender ID.

In this case, your SMS will be sent from one of our default short numbers:

- 38082 for transactional SMS messages

- 36047 for marketing SMS messages
