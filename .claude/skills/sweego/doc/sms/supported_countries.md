# What are supported countries?

Source : https://learn.sweego.io/docs/sms/supported_countries

> We support sending SMS in countries around the world, with the exception of 26 countries. These include at-risk countries.

We support sending SMS in countries around the world, with the exception of 26 countries. These include at-risk countries.

At-risk countries are often identified due to the prevalence of fraudulent activities, which can include Phishing (or Smishing, referring specifically to SMS). This involves fraudulent messages attempting to steal sensitive information. In countries with high incidences of these activities, businesses and consumers are more vulnerable to scams.

For this reason, we don’t allow the sending of SMS in the following countries:

- Algeria

- Azerbaijan

- Bangladesh

- Belarus

- Bhutan

- Guam

- Indonesia

- Iran

- Iraq

- Jordan

- Kazakhstan

- Kyrgyzstan

- Libya

- Madagascar

- Pakistan

- Russia

- Sri Lanka

- Syria

- Tajikistan

- Tunisia

- Uzbekistan

- Yemen
