# Sms templates

Source : https://learn.sweego.io/docs/sms/sms_templates

> With Sweego, you can create SMS templates to streamline your messaging process.

With Sweego, you can create SMS templates to streamline your messaging process.

To create an SMS template, follow these steps:

- Go to the [**Templates**](https://app.sweego.io/templates) section in the app.

- Click on **SMS** to begin creating your template.

![smstemplate1](undefined)

Click on **Create SMS template.**

![smstemplate1](undefined)

Next, complete the form with message details:

![smstemplate1](undefined)

- **Template Name**: Enter a name for your template.

- **Sender ID**: Choose the Sender ID you've defined on Sweego.

- **Message**: Enter your content. You can include emojis, URLs and more. Below, you’ll see the number of SMS that will be deducted for this message (per recipient) and the number of characters left for the current SMS.

A preview of your SMS will be visible on the right side of the screen. We shorten URLs in the preview but you can choose not to shorten them when you send your SMS.

Click on **Create SMS Template** to save your template.

![smstemplate1](undefined)

You can now send a test SMS using this template.

On the SMS template page, you'll see the template you just created. Click on the '...' menu on the right, then select "Send Test."

![smstemplate1](undefined)

Please note that sending a test SMS will deduct the cost of the message from your credit balance.

You can retrieve SMS logs on [https://app.sweego.io/logs/sms](https://app.sweego.io/logs/sms)

![smstemplate1](undefined)

You can find your template id on the home page. Go to how to send your SMS template via API
