# Sms statistics

Source : https://learn.sweego.io/docs/statistics/sms_statistics

> You can retrieve your statistics using the Sweego App interface

You can retrieve your statistics using the Sweego App interface

![Statistics](undefined)

Sms states are:

- Delivered: The SMS has been successfully received on the recipient's phone. This confirms that the phone is powered on and the message has been delivered.

- Rejected: The SMS was rejected by the operator (e.g., Free, Orange, Bouygues). This means the message could not be delivered due to operator-specific issues or restrictions.

- Failed: The message could not be delivered and will not be delivered. This status indicates a permanent delivery failure.

- Stopped: A "STOP" SMS is an incoming message from a recipient who wishes to unsubscribe. When a recipient sends "STOP" in their message, it indicates they no longer want to receive further SMS communications.
