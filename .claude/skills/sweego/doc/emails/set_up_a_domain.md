# Set up a domain

Source : https://learn.sweego.io/docs/emails/set_up_a_domain

> To start sending emails, you’ll have to set up and verify a domain.

To start sending emails, you’ll have to set up and verify a domain.

Before getting started, make sure you know who your hosting provider is, that you have access to their platform, and the permissions necessary to change records. If you don’t, figure out who in your organization might be able to do this for you.

![Hello](undefined)

From the Home screen, click on “Setup email.” You will arrive the Domains page, the first page of three to get your email channel up and working.

Click on the “Add new domain” button.

![domain](undefined)

A panel will open. Enter your valid subdomain name here.

**Tips: For good deliverability practices, it's recommanded to use a subdomain dedicated to sending your emails.**

![add_domain](undefined)

When you click on “Add new domain” at the bottom of the panel, you will be taken to a second panel to verify your domain.

![verify_domain](undefined)

In this panel, you’ll find the values necessary to configure domains on your hosting platform.

Go to your hosting platform and create these two CNAME records. The values that you need to enter can be copied with just one click from our panel.

Next, click the “Verify domain” button in the panel.

If your domain can be verified, we will notify you and redirect you to the domain list. You'll see that your newly added domain has a “Verified” status.

If your domain cannot be verified, we will also notify you and redirect you to the domain list. You'll find the unverified domain in your domain list with the status “Not verified”. You can always return later to verify the domain.

Depending on your hosting platform, it may take from a few minutes to several hours to verify your domain after making the necessary changes to records."

![domain_page](undefined)

Once you've finished verifying your domain, the next step will be to create an SMTP Access or an API Key.
