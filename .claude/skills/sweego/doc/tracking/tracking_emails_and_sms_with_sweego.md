# Why tracking emails and sms?

Sweego offers a comprehensive solution for tracking emails and SMS sent through the platform. This feature helps measure recipient engagement and optimize communication campaigns.

---

## Tracking Features

### Emails

With Sweego, you can track two main types of interactions:

- **Opens**: Indicates if a recipient has opened an email.

- **Clicks**: Tracks if a recipient has clicked on a link within the email.

### Definitions

- **Open**:
  
  An open is recorded when a recipient views an email containing an invisible tracking image (a tracking pixel). This method relies on the email client downloading the image.
  
  
  
  
  **Key considerations**:
  
  
  
  
  
  - If a recipient disables images in their email client, the open might not be tracked.
  
  - Email previews in some software might sometimes count as opens.

- **Click**:
  
  A click is recorded when a recipient clicks on a link in the email. Sweego redirects these clicks through a tracking URL that logs:
  
  
  
  
  
  - The date and time of the click.
  
  - Whether the click is human or generated by a proxy.

---

## Available Reports

- **Open rate**: Expressed as the percentage of emails opened compared to the total emails sent.

- **Click rate**: Expressed as the percentage of clicks compared to the total emails sent.

---

## Benefits of Tracking

- **Performance analysis**:
  
  Measure the effectiveness of your campaigns with accurate engagement data.

- **Smart segmentation**:
  
  Identify the most active segments of your recipients (those who frequently open or click).

- **Content optimization**:
  
  Adjust your messages to maximize engagement (e.g., improving subject lines to increase opens).

---

## Limitations and Precautions

- **Open tracking**:
  
  Open tracking relies on the tracking pixel and won’t work if the recipient’s email client blocks images.

- **False positives**:
  
  Some preview tools (e.g., Gmail or Outlook) may falsely record opens.

- **Click tracking**:
  
  Click tracking depends on URL redirections through the tracking URLs. This process can sometimes be affected by third-party tools or network restrictions.

---

## Conclusion

Sweego's tracking capabilities provide invaluable insights to optimize your campaigns and improve engagement. However, it is essential to consider the limitations of tracking technology when interpreting the results.
