# What is Sweego

Source : https://learn.sweego.io/docs/intro

Sweego is a unified API that allows you to send messages easily to your users. For now, Sweego allows you to send emails, but will soon allow you to send messages across multiple channels. Our solution is created for and by developers so you can send a message in just a few lines of code.

We are a French, GDPR-compliant solution. With Sweego, you’ll have peace of mind knowing your data is hosted in France. Your data is never sent outside the European Union.

Moreover, we strive to eco-design our solution. At each step of the design process, we ask ourselves what impact our product will have on the environment. Our goal is to make the lowest possible environmental impact possible, while meeting our customers’ needs.
