## The Problem

You receive a downtime notification, and you’d like to know more about what exactly happened.

The manual solution would be to open your HetrixTools dashboard, locate the affected uptime monitor, dig through its Location Fail Log and Network Diagnostics, as well as though its Server Metrics. This could take quite some time.

## The MCP Solution

Just ask your favorite AI to do it for you.

[![](https://docs.hetrixtools.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/3465242.png)](https://docs.hetrixtools.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/3465242.png)

And just like that, you’ve got a quick brief overview of what happened. In this case, we knew the outage was just a blip in our provider’s IPv4 connectivity and not a widespread transit issue.
