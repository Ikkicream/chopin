## The Problem

You’re managing a HetrixTools team account with 5+ [Sub-Account](https://docs.hetrixtools.com/sub-accounts/) team members, and you’d like an overview of the day’s team activity. Our [Activity Log](https://docs.hetrixtools.com/activity-log/) has very easy and capable search functionality, but going through hundreds or possibly thousands of log entries may be quite time-consuming.

## The MCP Solution

Just ask your favorite AI to do it for you.

[![](https://docs.hetrixtools.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2352342.png)](https://docs.hetrixtools.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2352342.png)

And just like that, you have a quick activity overview for the day. You can even schedule your favorite AI to create this report daily at the end of the working day.
